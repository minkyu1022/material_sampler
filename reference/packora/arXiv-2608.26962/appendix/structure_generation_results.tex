\section{Detailed Structure-Generation Results}
\label{app:structure-generation-results}

This appendix provides tabular results for the structure-generation benchmarks (\Cref{sec:benchmarks}) across candidate budgets and evaluation criteria.

\Cref{tab:benchmark_solc_main} reports the standard $\mathrm{Sol}_C$ and strict
$\mathrm{Sol}_C^{15/15}$ at budgets of 30 and 1{,}000 candidates, with
resampling standard deviations reported at the 30-candidate budget.
\Cref{tab:combined_all_metrics} provides additional collision, packing, and
recovery metrics for \model{}-M and \model{}-L at the matched 30-candidate
budget.
